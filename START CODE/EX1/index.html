Introduction to Kpop idol

A K-pop (Korean pop) idol is a professional music artist or group managed by a South Korean entertainment agency.

Similar to Western pop groups, idols are known for catchy music, synchronized dancing, high-production music videos, and intense connection with fans.

Concepts of K-pop Idols

The Trainee System : Before debuting, prospective idols spend years as "trainees" inside companies like SM, JYP, YG, or HYBE. They practice singing, dancing, rapping, and foreign languages for hours every day.

Debut and Promotion: After years of training, idols debut with a single or album. They promote their music through live performances, variety shows, and social media.

Fan Culture: K-pop idols have a strong connection with their fans, often referred to as "fandoms." Fans support idols through streaming music, attending concerts, and participating in fan events.

for example, BTS has a massive global fanbase known as Army.
